# Q4 – NotebookLM Evaluation

## Assessment

Assessment 3 – Robotic Process Automation, AI and Cloud-Enabled ESG Analytics

## Purpose

This repository documents the evaluation of Google NotebookLM as an AI-powered academic research assistant.

The evaluation uses an ESG academic research scenario in which a student analyses sustainability and climate-related university documents.

## Sources

The experiment used three publicly available ESG-related documents:

1. UNSW Environmental Sustainability Report 2023
2. University of Melbourne Sustainability Survey 2024
3. University of Sydney Climate-related Financial Disclosure 2024

## NotebookLM Capabilities Evaluated

The following capabilities were tested:

- Source-based question answering
- Document summarisation
- Cross-source comparison
- Study guide generation
- Evidence verification
- Identification of information gaps

## Key Findings

NotebookLM was effective for:

- Quickly identifying ESG themes
- Summarising long documents
- Comparing information across sources
- Supporting student revision
- Locating evidence that could be manually verified

Human verification remains necessary for important academic claims because AI-generated outputs can omit context or simplify complex information.

## Files

- `experiment_log.md` – prompts, experiment purposes and observed results
- `results.md` – consolidated evaluation findings

## Ethical Considerations

The evaluation recognises the importance of source verification, responsible AI use, academic judgement, privacy and avoiding over-reliance on AI-generated content.
