# Q4 NotebookLM Evaluation – Results

## Feature Evaluation

| NotebookLM Capability | Academic Use | Observed Result | Overall Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source-based Q&A | Finding information across multiple readings | Identified major ESG themes with source references | Useful |
| Summarisation | Preparing research briefs | Produced a structured summary of major arguments and evidence | Useful |
| Source comparison | Comparing different reports | Identified common priorities, differences and gaps | Highly useful |
| Study guide | Exam and revision preparation | Generated concepts, terminology and revision questions | Useful |
| Evidence verification | Checking factual claims | Provided claims that could be checked against source documents | Useful but requires human verification |

## Accuracy

NotebookLM generally produced relevant answers that were grounded in the uploaded sources. The factual verification experiment demonstrated that claims could be checked against the original documents.

However, AI-generated content should not automatically be treated as authoritative. Students should verify important facts, figures and interpretations against the original source.

## Relevance

The outputs were directly relevant to an ESG academic briefing. NotebookLM reduced the time required to identify themes, summarise documents and compare organisational approaches.

## Limitations

Potential limitations include:

- AI-generated summaries may omit important context.
- Complex ESG information can be simplified.
- Source interpretation may not always reflect the intended meaning.
- Users may become over-reliant on AI-generated summaries.
- Important claims should be checked against the original documents.
- Academic judgement and critical evaluation remain necessary.

## Conclusion

NotebookLM appears feasible as a supporting academic research and study tool. It is particularly useful for organising and synthesising multiple documents, but it should be used as a decision-support and learning aid rather than a replacement for independent academic analysis.
