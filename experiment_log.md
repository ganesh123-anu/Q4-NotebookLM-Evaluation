# Q4 NotebookLM Evaluation – Experiment Log

## Assessment
Assessment 3 – Robotic Process Automation, AI and Cloud-Enabled ESG Analytics

## Question
Q4 – Evaluating Cloud-Based Technology

## Evaluation Scenario
NotebookLM was evaluated as an AI-powered academic research assistant for preparing an ESG-related academic briefing.

## Sources Used

1. UNSW Environmental Sustainability Report 2023
2. University of Melbourne Sustainability Survey 2024
3. University of Sydney Climate-related Financial Disclosure 2024

## Experiment 1 – Source-Based Question Answering

Prompt:

"Based only on the uploaded sources, identify the three most important ESG themes discussed across the documents. For each theme, provide a concise explanation and cite the supporting source. Do not introduce information that is not supported by the uploaded sources."

Purpose:
To evaluate whether NotebookLM can identify important themes from multiple uploaded academic and professional sources while grounding its response in the supplied evidence.

Result:
NotebookLM identified climate action and net-zero transition, resource efficiency and waste minimisation, and sustainable governance, procurement, equity and inclusion as major themes.

## Experiment 2 – Summarisation

Prompt:

"Summarise the uploaded ESG materials for a university student preparing an academic briefing. Identify the main arguments, important evidence and key conclusions. Use only information supported by the uploaded sources and cite the relevant sources."

Purpose:
To evaluate the usefulness of NotebookLM for creating a concise academic briefing from multiple documents.

Result:
NotebookLM produced a structured summary covering strategic context, climate action, resource efficiency, governance and key conclusions.

## Experiment 3 – Source Comparison

Prompt:

"Compare the ESG priorities presented in the uploaded sources. Identify areas of agreement, differences in emphasis, and any important gaps. Present the comparison in a clear table and cite the relevant sources."

Purpose:
To test whether NotebookLM can synthesise information across different documents.

Result:
NotebookLM identified common ESG priorities while also highlighting differences in environmental measurement, governance and reporting emphasis.

## Experiment 4 – Study Guide

Prompt:

"Create a study guide based only on the uploaded sources. Include the key concepts, important terminology, five revision questions and concise answers. Ensure that the answers are supported by the uploaded sources."

Purpose:
To evaluate NotebookLM as a study and revision assistant.

Result:
NotebookLM generated a study-oriented structure containing key concepts, terminology and revision questions based on the uploaded sources.

## Experiment 5 – Evidence Verification

Prompt:

"Using only the uploaded sources, identify one specific factual claim from each source that could be verified directly from the document. For each claim, provide the source and explain where the supporting evidence can be found."

Purpose:
To evaluate whether NotebookLM provides source-grounded information that can be manually verified.

Result:
NotebookLM identified specific factual claims and indicated where supporting evidence could be located within the uploaded reports.

## Experiment 6 – Missing or Unclear Information

Prompt:

"Based only on the uploaded sources, identify any ESG information that is missing or unclear across the documents. Do not infer or invent information that is not explicitly provided."

Purpose:
To test whether NotebookLM can recognise information gaps instead of filling missing information with unsupported assumptions.

Result:
The output identified limitations relating to data availability, supply-chain information, reporting boundaries and methodological changes.

## Overall Observation

NotebookLM was useful for source-grounded research, summarisation, comparison and study preparation. However, outputs should still be checked against the original documents because AI-generated summaries may omit context, simplify complex evidence or require human verification.
